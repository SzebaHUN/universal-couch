# Community Guidelines

These apply to public rooms, this GitHub project and the community Discord server once it opens. Keep it simple:

- **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate, threats or bullying of other players.
- **No piracy.** Don't share, request or link ROMs, BIOS files or other pirated content.
- **Protect privacy.** Don't post room passwords, private invite links or other people's personal information.
- **Play fair in public rooms.** The host decides who plays and who watches.
- **Report problems.** Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/SzebaHUN/universal-couch/issues) for bugs and problems.

Breaking these guidelines may lead to removal from public rooms or the community. Discord's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines also apply on Discord.
